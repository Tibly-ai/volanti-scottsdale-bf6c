VOLANTI SCOTTSDALE sits on the runway side of the airport. Lunch and dinner with the taxiway in view, wine lockers inside, a hangar for events, and a helicopter deck just outside the glass.

01 Dine

A dining room with a view of the taxiway.

You eat with aircraft rolling past the windows. The kitchen runs lunch straight through dinner, the bar is built to sit at, and the wine program goes deep enough that regulars keep their own bottles here.

Next door, the hangar opens up for private events. Outside, the deck is where the helicopters wait. One address covers a quiet weekday lunch, a long table for forty, and a flight over the valley before dessert.

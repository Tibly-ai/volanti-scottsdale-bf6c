Members’ Cellar

Wine Lockers

Keep your own wine at the restaurant. Reserve a locker, fill it with bottles you actually want to drink, and have them opened at your table whenever you come in for lunch, dinner or a long afternoon watching aircraft come and go.

A locker program at VOLANTI SCOTTSDALE, on the runway at Scottsdale Airport.

Your cellar, waiting at your table

Regulars told us the same thing for years: they wanted their wine here, not at home. So we built lockers into the dining room. Members store bottles with us, and the staff knows exactly which ones are yours.

Some members keep a steady rotation of everyday reds. Others set aside something special for an anniversary, a closed deal, or the night a friend finally flies in. Either way, the bottle is here when you sit down, and there is nothing to carry in or out.

Lockers are limited by the space we have, so availability moves. Our team will tell you what is open, what the program costs, and how storage works before you commit to anything.

What members get

The program, piece by piece

The locker

Space of your own

A dedicated locker inside the restaurant, held in your name and kept under our care rather than in a back room you never see.

The wine

Bottles you pick

Stock it with wine you want to drink. Our team can talk through what is on our list if you would rather build the locker with us.

The service

Poured at your table

Tell your server which bottle you are in the mood for. It arrives opened, at temperature, with the right glassware on the table.

The terms

Stated up front

Membership length, fees, storage limits and house policy on your wine are all covered before you sign. Ask us anything first.

How it works

Four steps from first ask to first pour

01

Ask about availability

Lockers open up as members come and go. Call or email the restaurant and we will tell you what is free right now and what the current terms are.

02

Fill the locker

Bring in the bottles you want on hand, or build the locker from our wine list with help from the team. Your name goes on it and the wine stays with us.

03

Come in and drink it

Book a table, mention your locker, and the bottle you want is opened for you. No corkage conversation, no carrying wine through the parking lot.

04

Restock when it runs low

Top the locker back up whenever you like. Members often add a bottle or two each visit so there is always something waiting for the next one.

Start a locker

Come see the lockers in person

The easiest way to join is to sit down with us. Book a table, ask for the wine locker program, and we will show you what is open and walk through the terms over a glass.

Experiences

The runway is only the beginning. Keep your bottles on site in a private locker, lift off from the deck for a tour over the valley, book the hangar for a party, or send a full meal to the aircraft. Pick the one you came for.

One address,
several ways in

Volanti sits on the field at Scottsdale Airport, which is why a dinner here can turn into something else entirely. Guests come for lunch and leave with a wine locker. Others book a table and add a helicopter tour before dessert. Private groups take the hangar.

Each experience has its own page with the details, and our team can arrange more than one for the same evening. Tell us what you have in mind when you reserve.

Choose your experience

  • 01

    Wine Lockers

    Keep your own bottles at the restaurant in a private, climate-controlled locker and have them poured whenever you sit down. A standing table for the regulars who would rather drink their own cellar.

    Wine Lockers
  • 02

    Helicopter Tour

    Lift off from the field and see Scottsdale from the air, then come back down to the table. Pair it with lunch or dinner, or fly on its own.

    Helicopter Tour
  • 03

    Hangar Events

    Private parties, receptions and corporate nights staged in the hangar beside the restaurant. Details and inquiries live on our events site.

    Hangar Events
  • 04

    Jet Catering

    Food from our kitchen delivered to the aircraft. Orders and the full catering menu are handled through Volanti Jet Catering.

    Jet Catering
  • 05

    Run the Runway

    The run held on the runway at Scottsdale Airport. Registration and event information are on the Run the Runway site.

    Run the Runway
